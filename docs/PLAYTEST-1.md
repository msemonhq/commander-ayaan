# Playtest Guide: Phase 1 (For Ayaan's Co-Play Session)

## What to Watch While Ayaan Plays
1. **The First 10 Seconds (Wordless Start)**: Does Ayaan start without any adult prompt? Does he notice the glowing Sun and follow the translucent ghost hand tapping it? Record exact seconds before his first touch.
2. **Station Door Recognition**: In the Hub, does he recognize the two live preview doors (the spinning planet toy vs the hopping runway planets) without any words or labels? Which station does he touch first?
3. **Planet Toys in Playground**: Which toys does he return to repeatedly? Does he laugh when Venus spins backwards, or when Jupiter lines up 11 Earths? Does he hold the Sun to pour Earths in?
4. **Runway Order (Planet Parade)**: Does he drag or tap-then-tap? On early rungs (R1-R2), do the faint silhouettes help him match before he learns the order?
5. **Wordless Hints & Mistakes**: When he misses a slot, does the soft "boop", the gentle wobble, and the ghost hand demonstrating the move keep him calm and motivated? Does he wait for the ghost hand when unsure?

## Top 5 Things I Am Least Sure About
1. **Wordless Hub Station Discovery**: Whether Ayaan immediately understands that the two animated round portals lead to games without reading labels.
2. **Ghost Hand Mimicry**: Whether a 6-year-old intuitively mirrors the ghost hand's dragging motion or prefers tap-then-tap placement.
3. **Venus Reverse Spin Clarity**: Whether he notices Venus is spinning opposite to his drag finger without an adult pointing it out.
4. **Small-Screen Tray vs Runway Density**: On compact phones (360x740), whether the 72dp targets give enough physical clearance around his fingers.
5. **Constellation Progress**: Whether he notices the 5-star constellation drawing star by star across the top, or only pays attention to the runway slots.
