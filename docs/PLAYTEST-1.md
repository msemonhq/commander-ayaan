# Playtest Guide: Phase 1 (For Ayaan's Co-Play Session)

## What to Watch While Ayaan Plays
1. **The First 10 Seconds**: Notice whether the rising Sun and floating Orbi on the Boot screen naturally invite a tap without needing verbal instructions.
2. **Exploration Instinct**: In Meet the Planets, does he tap planets randomly or systematically? Does he notice each planet's signature movement (e.g., Earth's Moon, Saturn's wobbling ring, Mars dusting)?
3. **Runway Order (Planet Parade)**: In Rung 1 and Rung 2, does he understand that the slots lead outward from the Sun? Observe whether he prefers tap-then-tap or dragging.
4. **Hint Ladder Reaction**: When he places a planet in an incorrect slot, notice if the gentle wobble, boop tone, and Orbi's guidance keep him calm and curious rather than frustrated.
5. **Session Wrap-up**: Does he smile at the completion stars, or does he immediately try another round?

## Top 5 Things I Am Least Sure About
1. **Tray vs Runway Spacing on Compact Screens (360x740)**: Whether 6-year-old fingers accidentally tap adjacent slots when runway targets are clustered near the bottom tray.
2. **Reading vs Icon Reliance**: While every spoken instruction has large icons and Orbi subtitles, whether Ayaan waits for the audio cue or taps ahead.
3. **Dot Cues on Rung 1**: Whether the dot counts (1, 2, 3) on the slots are immediately understood as 1st, 2nd, 3rd from the Sun without explanation.
4. **Planet Discrimination**: Can he instantly tell Uranus apart from Neptune on a smaller screen based on tilt and ring, or does he rely on color alone?
5. **Tap-then-Tap Comprehension**: Whether a child naturally realizes tapping a floating tray planet "selects" it and tapping an empty runway slot "places" it.
